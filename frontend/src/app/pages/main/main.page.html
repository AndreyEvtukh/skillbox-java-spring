Hotel Booking

book your stay quickly and easily

Explore available rooms, check their details and availability, and choose the dates that work best for you. Create an account to make bookings and manage your reservations.

Hotel Booking is a full-stack hotel reservation platform that allows users to search for hotels, explore available rooms, check room details, and make bookings for a selected period.

The platform provides hotel search and filtering by rating, room availability, booking management, and user ratings from 1 to 5. Users can choose a suitable room, select their dates, and manage their reservations.
